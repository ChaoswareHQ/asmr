is cited for a specific technique identifier or vulnerability, the identifier is
given.

## Note on citation practice

Where a case study quotes a command, a technique identifier, or a vulnerability
identifier, the primary source is the MITRE or NVD entry listed above, or the
public disclosure cited. Where a case study uses a scenario (Chapter 39) rather
than a named public incident, no reference is claimed, and the text says so.
